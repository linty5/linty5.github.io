  • A sudden change alone does not mean a new shot: what separates a true boundary from a pseudo-event is whether the change lasts.
  • By testing this, PERSIST roughly halves the pseudo-event false alarms of TransNetV2 at the same recall and stays highly competitive on four benchmarks.

How it works

Learning a persistence criterion.

Local change provides evidence. A continuous latent state helps distinguish lasting transitions from changes that soon return.

During training, PERSIST learns whether a visual change lasts or the video returns to its earlier appearance. This helps it distinguish new shots from brief disturbances.

Evaluation

What changes in the prediction?

The model learns to separate transient disturbances from real cuts, while remaining competitive across four video domains.
